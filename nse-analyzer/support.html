Common questions

QuestionAnswer
The menu does not appear.Reload the spreadsheet, then look under Extensions > NSE Analyzer.
"Sheet is missing. Run Setup."Choose Setup / repair sheets. It recreates missing sheets and keeps your data.
Data looks stale.Sheets refresh hourly during market hours (09:15-15:30 IST); the open dashboard refreshes every minute. Outside market hours you see the last published values. The status line shows the data time.
The first load is slow.NSE's servers often drop sessions, so the add-on retries. The first setup can take a minute or two.
Gemini says the model is not found.Run AI (Gemini) > Test Gemini connection; it lists the models your key can use. Put one in Settings > GeminiModel.
A strategy shows ERROR in the Check column.The message names the problem in the condition. The strategy language is listed on the Strategies sheet.
How do I remove the add-on and my data?See Retention and deletion.
